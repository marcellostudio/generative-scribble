# Generative Scribble

**Shaped by randomness. Guided by interaction.**

An interactive generative animation designed and developed by Marcello Di Giovanni.

## Overview

Generative Scribble is an evolving, interactive line that lives in the hero section of [marcello.design](https://www.marcello.design/).

More than a visual element, it represents a design philosophy where structure and freedom, logic and imagination, control and randomness coexist.

## The Concept

The line moves autonomously, following a generative behavior shaped by randomness. Its path is unpredictable, constantly evolving and never exactly the same.

But when someone interacts with it, randomness gives way to intention.

Visitors can take control, guide the movement and create their own path.

This transition from autonomous behavior to human interaction reflects the relationship between people and technology.

Algorithms generate possibilities. Human creativity gives them meaning and purpose.

## Design Philosophy

Generative Scribble challenges the idea that every aspect of design must be perfectly planned or controlled.

Sometimes, the most interesting outcomes emerge from experimentation, imperfection and the unexpected.

The project explores how code, technology and AI can expand what we imagine, explore and create, rather than simply automate creativity.
